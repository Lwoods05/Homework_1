What fuels my creativity

Inspiration

I am inspired by african american superheroes becuase to me the inspired me to break the limits on what I feel I can achieve and accomplish, strong communities with different perspectives ethics that stayed strong and overcame hardships, and the idea that creative work can make a positive difference for everyone around you and can also help inspire someone else to do something great and to help them to grow in their own creativity and to help them to be the best that they can be.

Superheroes

Superheroes inspire me because they represent courage, service, and helping others.

Community

I am motivated by the people around me and the power of sharing stories together.

Purpose

My goal is to create work that brings joy, encourages growth, and leaves a positive mark.
